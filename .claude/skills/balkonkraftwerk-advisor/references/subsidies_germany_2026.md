# Balkonkraftwerk Subsidies in Germany (2026)

## Important: how subsidy logic works

Most municipal programs require **application BEFORE purchase**. Buying first and then applying usually disqualifies you. The few exceptions (Trier, some others) explicitly state "Antrag nach Inbetriebnahme."

The standard workflow:
1. Apply online via the city's funding portal
2. Wait for preliminary approval (Vorbescheid)
3. Buy and install
4. Submit Verwendungsnachweis (final invoice + MaStR confirmation)
5. Receive payment to your account

**Always check the current status of the funding pot.** Programs often pause when budget is exhausted, then restart in the next budget cycle.

## NRW (Nordrhein-Westfalen)

NRW has no statewide program — funding is via individual cities.

- **Köln**: 150–200 € flat rate per Wohneinheit via "Klimafreundliches Wohnen" program. Up to 300 € for Köln-Pass holders. Battery storage for plug-in systems explicitly excluded. Check status at koeln.de — payouts have been periodically paused.
- **Bonn**: Up to 800 €/kWp — significantly more generous than Köln
- **Düsseldorf**: 50% of acquisition costs, max 600 €. For Düsselpass holders: 80% / max 800 € with energy consultation
- **Erftkreis cities (Kerpen, Hürth, Bergheim, Pulheim, Frechen, etc.)**: 200 € pauschal
- **Coesfeld**: Restarting March 2026, last round was 100 € flat or up to 540 € (90%) for low-income
- **Münster, Aachen, Essen, Dortmund**: Check current status — programs change frequently

## Berlin

**SolarPLUS program**: up to 500 € including for battery storage. One of the most generous in Germany. Battery storage explicitly eligible.

## Bayern

- **München**: Klima- und Umweltschutz program, proportional subsidy. Antrag must be filed BEFORE order. Self-built systems excluded.

## Baden-Württemberg

- **Stuttgart**: 200 € flat, 300 € for Bonuscard+Kultur holders
- **Ulm**: up to 100 €, max 50% of cost
- **Walldorf, Heidelberg, Karlsruhe**: varying programs, check current status
- **Freiburg**: 150 € flat / 300 € for Freiburg-Pass holders — **tenants only**. Battery storage no longer eligible since June 2025

## Hessen

- **Frankfurt am Main**: program varies, check current
- **Darmstadt**: 200 € for 1-module setup (200–450 W), 400 € for 2-module setup (≥450 W). Max 50% of cost
- **Kassel**: 150 € flat, application BEFORE purchase

## Sachsen

State-level program for tenant contingents currently active. Plus city-level:
- **Leipzig**: 1:1 cost match up to 300 € for pass holders
- **Dresden, Chemnitz**: check current

## Niedersachsen

- **Hannover**: via proKlima fund, varying amounts

## Hamburg

Since October 2025, Hamburg's program is **exclusively for low-income households** (Bürgergeld, Sozialhilfe, Wohngeld, BAföG, or income under Pfändungsfreigrenze). Up to 90% of cost as Sachleistung via Caritas. TÜV-certified modules required.

## Rheinland-Pfalz

- **Trier**: 200 € flat, program ends 31 March 2026. Application AFTER commissioning (unusual)
- **Mainz, Ludwigshafen**: check current

## Schleswig-Holstein, Bremen, Saarland, Brandenburg, Sachsen-Anhalt, Thüringen, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern

Mostly city-level, smaller and intermittent. Mecklenburg-Vorpommern has active state-level tenant contingents.

## How to check current status

The reliable approach for any city:
1. Search `Stadt [name] Balkonkraftwerk Förderung 2026` 
2. Find the official municipal page (look for `.koeln.de`, `.muenchen.de`, etc.)
3. Check "aktueller Fördertopf" or "Stand des Programms"
4. Call the Klimaschutz or Umweltamt office for confirmation if the website is ambiguous

Don't rely on aggregator sites for current pot status — they often lag by months.

## What's NOT a subsidy but acts like one

- **0% VAT** under § 12 Abs. 3 UStG — saves 19% on hardware automatically
- **EEG-Einspeisevergütung** — technically available but practically useless for Balkonkraftwerk (most Netzbetreiber don't offer contracts for this size, and the ~8 ct/kWh wouldn't justify the bureaucracy)

## Stacking rules

City + 0% VAT can usually be stacked. Some programs explicitly require you to use the 0% VAT rate. Check the specific program's terms.

Federal + city: there's no current federal program for Balkonkraftwerke, so stacking isn't an issue today.
