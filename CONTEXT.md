# Helios — Bounded Context

## Glossary

- **Helios**: Chat-first AI assistant that fronts an epilot journey end-to-end. The customer talks; Helios handles product selection and bureaucracy.
- **Balkonkraftwerk**: Plug-in balcony solar system (Steckersolargerät). The demo use case is a Cologne Balkonkraftwerk purchase.
- **Journey**: An epilot customer-facing flow (research → decide → buy). Helios replaces the form-based journey with a conversational one.
- **Tail pivot**: A 30-second demo moment where the customer asks about a different product category (EV wallbox), proving the architecture generalizes beyond Balkonkraftwerk.
- **Feasibility**: The phase where Helios assesses whether a Balkonkraftwerk makes sense for the customer's specific balcony (dimensions, orientation, shading, railing type).
- **Self-consumption rate (Eigenverbrauch)**: The fraction of generated solar power actually used in the household vs. fed into the grid. Ranges from ~30% (no battery) to ~95% (battery + smart meter).
- **Nulleinspeisung**: Zero feed-in — a smart meter tells the battery to match real-time consumption so nothing is wasted to the grid.
- **MaStR (Marktstammdatenregister)**: Federal registry where all solar installations must be registered within one month of commissioning.
- **WEG (Wohnungseigentümergemeinschaft)**: Owner's association in apartment buildings. Tenants must notify landlord/WEG before facade-altering installations.
